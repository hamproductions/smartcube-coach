## 5. Methodology and decision record

1. Remote controller is deliberately state-agnostic: every accepted turn advances target playback regardless of cube state or move identity. It is not scramble verification.
2. `RemoteControllerSession.handleMove()` produces target index/completion. `RemoteTrainerController.onProgress` lifts index into `TrainerHub`; `CubePlayback.playbackIndex` stops autonomous playback, clamps index, and renders a prefix algorithm in `twisty-player`.
3. Before recognition/hints, title is `Controller playback`; manual controls, stage buttons, and move stream are hidden. This blocks case/algorithm leakage through the actual player.
4. Completion chooses next case and resets timer, recognition/hints/answer/index. `key={current.id}` is necessary; without it the next player inherited previous move index.
5. Browser proof is separate from tests. Screenshot visibly contains the real 3D cube but cannot prove real BLE delivery.
